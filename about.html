Core Expertise

Deep experience in digital infrastructure.

Domain Hosting Management

We manage the complete lifecycle of your domain and hosting environment — from registration and DNS configuration to server optimization and uptime monitoring.

  • Domain registration, transfer, and renewal management
  • DNS configuration and advanced record management
  • Shared, VPS, and dedicated hosting setup and migration
  • SSL certificate installation and renewal
  • Uptime monitoring, backups, and security hardening
  • Email deliverability and DNS authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC)

Microsoft License Purchasing & Maintenance

We help businesses navigate Microsoft's licensing landscape — ensuring you have the right licenses, at the right price, with ongoing support and compliance.

  • Microsoft 365 Business & Enterprise license procurement
  • License optimization and cost reduction analysis
  • Tenant setup, user provisioning, and migration
  • Exchange Online, SharePoint, and Teams configuration
  • Ongoing license management and renewal tracking
  • Compliance auditing and security baseline configuration
